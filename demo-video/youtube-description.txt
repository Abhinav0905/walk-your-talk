Walk Your Talk turns the five beats of a talk into five odd objects placed on real furniture along a route through your room: a memory palace (method of loci) for SPECS.

Build: pinch each glowing spot and a beat drops onto the furniture as a vivid object.
Walk: walk up to each object; it wakes up and shows its beat just long enough to say it out loud, hands free and eyes up.
Recall: the objects hide behind question marks. Say each beat from memory, pinch to check, and grade yourself.
Debrief: see your first-pass score and practice a route of only the beats that slipped.

Planned with the Devpost Learn plan-lens skill. Built with CLAD, Lens Studio 5.23.2 and the Lens Studio MCP. Spectacles Interaction Kit and UIKit; objects from Lens Studio's 3D generator; sounds from CLAD's build-sfx. Validation: 10 logic tests and 7 LEAF scenarios pass in the preview.

These are recordings of the running Lens in Lens Studio's preview (Colorful Home room), driven by synthetic preview input. The room and spot positions are simulated; on glasses you would pinch your own furniture. Comfort and learning outcomes on real SPECS are not yet tested.

Method of loci study: Dresler et al., "Mnemonic Training Reshapes Brain Networks to Support Superior Memory", Neuron (2017).

Voice: the narration uses Kokoro TTS with the af_heart voice. It's a synthetic voice, not a clone of anyone's voice. The English captions were added separately.
